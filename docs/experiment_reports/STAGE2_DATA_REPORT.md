## 4. Qubit state at a decision

For each CCE the decision time is the poll that published it. Every other property is attached by an **as-of join on publication time**, so a row contains only information that existed at decision time. This is enforced by assertions.

State variables:
- T1 and T2 (µs)
- 1-qubit (sx) error
- readout error, P(0|1) and P(1|0)
- mean and max CZ error over the qubit's 1–3 couplings, plus the fraction of couplings at the failure ceiling
- age of each property at decision time
- gap since the previous CCE
- hours since the last chip-wide recalibration (unknown before the first observed event: 39 % missing, flagged)
- time-of-day encoding

Environmental covariates (8 columns) are kept for an ablation only. They are sparse: solar flux and Dst are 73 % missing, Bz 40 %, Kp/Ap 22 %.

Missing state values: T2 0.12 %, sx 1.1 %, CZ 0.6 %. Inputs are filled only from the same qubit's past, then by a constant. Missing **targets** stay NaN and are masked.

**Leakage guards (automated, all passing):**
- no property value calibrated after its decision time;
- CCEs strictly ordered;
- no qubit in both the development and held-out sets;
- every training (sample, horizon) pair has its decision before T_val;
- inputs contain no NaN;
- scalers and any other fitted quantity are fitted on training pairs only (Stage 3).

## 6. Data-quality findings

1. **Mixed T1/T2 units.** Seconds before 2026-05-07, µs from 2026-05-13 (schema change). Harmonised to µs. The same bug had invalidated the original pipeline.
2. **A collection gap during the schema change** (2026-05-07 → 05-13).
3. **Device event, mid-May 2026, on ibm_fez and ibm_kingston.**
   - T1 fell for 96–97 % of qubits (median per-qubit ratio 0.72 and 0.64), with a wide per-qubit spread (10–90 % range 0.63–0.83 and 0.57–0.78). T2 also fell.
   - ibm_marrakesh, which went through the same schema change, was unaffected (median ratio 0.97).
   - Recovery was gradual over about 3 weeks (back to 1.01× and 0.95× by mid-June).
   - **Interpretation:** a physical device-level event (cause not identifiable from the data), not a unit artifact, because a unit artifact would be a constant factor. It is in the training period and is relevant to H5 (abrupt change).
4. **T2 > 2·T1 in 0.27 % of states.** Physically impossible, so it is measurement noise. The raw values are kept; the simulator clamps T2 to at most 2·T1.
5. **Readout probabilities are quantised to 1/8192 (8,192 shots) and can be 0.** A floor of 1/16384 is applied before log transforms.

## 7. Limitations

- **The true device state between calibrations is unobservable.** The next published calibration is used as a proxy for the state at execution time.
- Calibration values are IBM's *estimates* and carry their own measurement noise. Part of the "drift" is estimation noise, which no forecaster can predict. This bounds achievable forecast accuracy.
- There are three Heron r2 devices at one site, so device-level generalisation is tested on a single unseen device.
- Environmental covariates are sparse and site-level (not fridge-level).
- About 8.5 months of data. Seasonal effects cannot be separated.
